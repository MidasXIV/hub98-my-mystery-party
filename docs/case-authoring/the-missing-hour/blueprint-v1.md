## Public premise (safe to adapt into the briefing)

The morning before Bellweather Community Archive's centenary exhibition, its
founding agreement is missing from a display awaiting installation. Five
volunteers' accounts seem to place it in three rooms during one evening hour.
An older exhibition photograph also calls the displayed document's identity
into question. Reconstruct what each person saw, distinguish the documents,
and determine where the original went.

The player is an external reviewer acting at the archive's request. The event
is being investigated immediately, not reopened as a cold case. No police
authority or wrongdoing is assumed.

## Claims, contradictions, and explanation

- Established: no theft, deliberate concealment, forged evidence, unreliable
  clock, or secret access route. All required movements occur in ordinary rooms.
- Mara's 'the agreement was ready' is a mistaken inference about identity and
  continued location, not an observed fact after 18:25.
- Evelyn and Theo describe different physical objects. Their observations are
  compatible once identity, time, mount, and reverse markings are separated.
- Isha's queue shorthand and Mara's unchecked checklist create a misleading
  administrative chain; both underlying item records remain available.
- Noah supplies his statement and cites CS-041 from the outset. The complete
  condition-record export arrives later as a designed evidence release. He is
  not withholding an answer to prolong the plot. His statement must explicitly
  say AG-01 stayed in the digitisation room and CS-041 records its final location.
- The original was transferred to labelled temporary storage; the reproduction
  was returned to preparation. Neither was at the gallery stand on opening day.
- The old photograph corroborates original identity, not the modern location.
  It must not be treated as a third contemporary sighting.

## Ordered objectives and availability proof

Dependencies below are authoring notes only. Runtime has an authored objective
array, not a supported DAG or a guarantee of server-enforced order. The
Integrator must preserve this order without inventing dependency fields.

### tmh_o01 — Distinguish the two documents

- Prompt: Which two physical documents were being called 'the agreement', and
  which features distinguish them?
- Available proof: `tmh_e07_inventory`, `tmh_e09_mount_record`,
  `tmh_e04_theo_statement`, `tmh_e05_evelyn_statement` (all initial).
- Expected discovery: AG-01 is unmounted cream paper with reverse pencil 6;
  RP-01 is white, grey-mounted, footer DISPLAY COPY / RP-01, blank reverse.
  A front crease alone is inconclusive because the copy reproduces it.
- Accept equivalent wording identifying both objects and at least one reliable
  distinguishing feature for each. Do not require memorised exact prose.
- Releases: `tmh_e12_old_display_record`, `tmh_e13_checklist_history`.

### tmh_o02 — Reconstruct the three sightings

- Prompt: What was seen at 18:12, 18:20, and 18:25, and where did the display
  copy go afterward? Explain whether the accounts conflict.
- Available proof: initial statements/inventory/mount record plus
  `tmh_e13_checklist_history`, released by completed `tmh_o01`.
- Expected discovery: Evelyn saw AG-01 in digitisation at 18:12; Theo moved
  RP-01 preparation → gallery at 18:20; Mara saw RP-01 there at 18:25;
  RP-01 returned to preparation returns tray at 18:32. No impossible movement
  or contradictory eyewitness account is necessary.
- Accept clear room/object/event reconstruction, not a culprit name.
- Releases: none.

### tmh_o03 — Identify the missing handoff record

- Prompt: Why does the signed display checklist fail to establish the original's
  location, and which named record and author should resolve it?
- Available proof: `tmh_e02_mara_statement`, `tmh_e06_noah_statement`,
  `tmh_e07_inventory`, `tmh_e08_display_checklist`, `tmh_e10_handoff_message`
  (initial), and `tmh_e13_checklist_history` (from `tmh_o01`).
- Expected discovery: Mara signed from a reproduction sighting without
  rechecking; the original's final location was never copied over. Request
  Noah's CS-041, the condition/transfer record for AG-01.
- Accept record identity (CS-041 or unambiguous equivalent), author, and
  explanation of the unchecked handoff. Do NOT demand the final storage location.
- Releases: `tmh_e14_condition_sheet`. It is the response to a justified record
  request, not proof used to earn its own unlock.

### tmh_o04 — Locate the original and explain the empty display

- Prompt: Locate AG-01 precisely, account for RP-01, and explain why the stand
  was empty without treating a mistaken checklist as proof of theft.
- Available proof: `tmh_e14_condition_sheet` (from `tmh_o03`), initial
  `tmh_e11_room_directory` and `tmh_e07_inventory`, plus
  `tmh_e13_checklist_history` (from `tmh_o01`).
- Expected discovery: AG-01 is in T-17, shelf 2, cabinet C in digitisation;
  RP-01 remains in preparation returns tray. Independent movements and a missed
  handoff left the stand empty. No additional perpetrator is required.
- Accept complete destinations and causal explanation. A record provides a
  documented location; physical recovery is confirmed only afterward.
- Releases: `tmh_e15_recovery_record` (epilogue, never prerequisite evidence).

The authored path is o01 → o02 → o03 → o04. o03 is independently answerable
after o01; this is a valid alternate deduction, not an unsupported runtime lock.
No objective may require proving completion of another objective in its answer.
Offline print can include all records; staged envelopes are optional facilitation,
not a confidentiality promise. A print reader may make discoveries out of order.
